Hero's electric brand Vida has crossed 2 lakh scooters in 2026, and there are still two months left on the calendar. According to [Autocar India](https://www.autocarindia.com/bike-news/hero-vida-sales-cross-2-lakh-units-in-2026-441181), Vida clocked 2,00,143 units between 1 January and 29 October 2026. In all of 2025, it managed 1,13,021. That is a jump of about 77%, before November has even started.

The Hero Vida 2 lakh milestone comes with a second record. October 2026 was Vida's best month ever, with 27,435 scooters.

## October beat September, which was also a record

Vida's previous best was 24,435 units in September, as per Autocar India's figures. October added 3,000 more on top of that, a 12.3% jump in a single month. Against October 2025 (16,499 units), the growth is 66%.

The more interesting part is how steady this has been. Vida grew every single quarter this year:

| Period | Units |
|---|---|
| Q1 2026 (Jan–Mar) | 49,043 |
| Q2 2026 (Apr–Jun) | 57,059 |
| Q3 2026 (Jul–Sep) | 66,606 |
| October 2026 (till 29 Oct) | 27,435 |

Add those up and you land exactly on 2,00,143. No single blockbuster month did the heavy lifting here. It's a staircase.

These numbers track retail registrations, not factory dispatches, so they reflect scooters that actually reached buyers.

## Where Vida stands in the e-scooter race

Vida now holds about 11% of India's electric two-wheeler market in 2026, up from 8% in the same period last year. That puts it at No. 4, behind TVS, Bajaj and Ather, and ahead of Ola Electric and Greaves.

In our September registrations story, Hero (Vida) posted the biggest month-on-month jump among the top five brands. October suggests that wasn't a one-off.

Back in August, Autocar India expected Vida to finish 2026 at around 2.25 lakh units. The estimate has now moved up to 2.5–2.6 lakh. When forecasts keep getting revised upward, a brand is doing something right.

## The factory is the next big test

Hero has doubled Vida's monthly production capacity at its Chittoor plant in Andhra Pradesh, from 15,000 units at the end of FY2026 to 30,000 units in September 2026. Hero MotoCorp CEO Harshavardhan Chitale has said the company expects it to reach 45,000 units a month by Q4 FY2027, according to Autocar India.

Here's why that matters. October's 27,435 units is already about 91% of the 30,000 monthly capacity (27,435 ÷ 30,000). If demand keeps climbing, the next jump in capacity can't come soon enough. That's the kind of problem every EV maker wants.

## What this means for you

If you are looking at a Vida VX2 or V2, the brand behind it is clearly not a side project anymore. Hero is putting real money into making more of these scooters, and that usually means better availability and wider service support in smaller towns.

The flip side: in a fast-growing month, popular colours and variants can see waiting periods. Check delivery timelines with your dealer before you lock in a booking.
