The [Ather Konarc](https://www.atherenergy.com/konarc) S 125 claims 2 km more range than the TVS iQube 3.1 kWh from 0.4 kWh less battery, and costs ₹10,739 less. ₹1,21,999 against ₹1,32,738, ex-showroom Bengaluru.

The iQube's answer is 12 km/h.

## Smaller battery, same range

|  | Ather Konarc S 125 | TVS iQube 3.1 kWh |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price (ex-showroom, Bengaluru) | ₹1,21,999 | ₹1,32,738 |
| Battery | 2.7 kWh | 3.1 kWh |
| Claimed range | 125 km (IDC) | 123 km (IDC) |
| Top speed | 70 km/h | 82 km/h |
| Motor | 4.7 kW, 16 Nm | 4.4 kW peak |
| Boot space | 31 litres | — |

This is the clearest illustration of what Ather's EL platform does. Less battery, same claimed range, lower price. A 2.7 kWh pack is also cheaper to replace out of warranty than a 3.1 kWh one, which matters in year six.

The iQube's 82 km/h is the counter. The Konarc S range is capped at 70 km/h, and that is its honest weak point against almost everything in this price band.

Ather's TrueRange figure for the S 125 is 100 km — the number to plan around.

## Charging

TVS supplies a portable charger with the iQube and quotes about 3 hours 30 minutes for 10-80% on the 3.1 kWh pack.

The Konarc's 450W charger is built into the scooter, so you travel with a cable instead of a brick. On its own, 0-80% is about 4 hours 30 minutes. With a 450W additional charger working alongside it, combined charging brings that to roughly 2 hours 25 minutes — faster than the iQube, but only if you buy the extra charger.

Budget for it. On the S 125 the additional charger is optional, not standard.

## DC fast charging

The Konarc can use Ather Grid, which crossed over 5,000 public fast chargers nationwide as of Ather's January 2026 network update, with up to 15 km of range claimed from a 10-minute stop.

TVS does not offer DC fast charging on the iQube.

This is the consistent story across every Konarc comparison, and it is the feature most buyers underrate until the first time they need it.

## TVS is not standing still

The iQube is the volume leader's product, not an also-ran. TVS shipped [71,382 electric two-wheelers in October](https://evgyan.com/blogs/tvs-ev-dispatches-october-2026), up 120% year on year, and leads Bajaj in monthly E2W registrations.

That scale buys you easy servicing, easy spares and strong resale — three things a spec sheet never shows.

## What this means for you

**If you want the better value and charge away from home sometimes:** take the Konarc S 125 — ₹10,739 less, a marginally longer claimed range, a bigger boot, and fast-charging access. Budget for the additional charger.

**If you regularly ride above 70 km/h:** take the iQube 3.1. Its 82 km/h top speed is 12 km/h more than the Konarc can ever offer.

**If TVS service is simply more convenient where you live:** that convenience alone can be worth ₹10,739 to a lot of riders.

12 km/h is worth that money to some and nothing at all to others. Only you know which one you are.
