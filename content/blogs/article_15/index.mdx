Electric trucks have long been the hardest part of India's EV transition to crack — heavy, expensive, and running routes where downtime is costly. This week's news suggests the segment is finally starting to move at real scale.

## JSW enters with Ampstar

On 28 October, **JSW Greentech** launched its new electric commercial vehicle brand, **Ampstar**. Right out of the gate, Ampstar secured an order for **100 trucks** from JSW Cement, and has built a **330 km battery-swapping corridor** to support them. A large industrial group entering with both the vehicles and the charging infrastructure built in-house, from day one, is a meaningfully different approach than startups trying to bolt on infra after the fact.

## A 5,000-truck deal worth up to ₹150 crore

Separately on 27 October, **BikeWo GreenTech** announced a partnership with **Green Drive Mobility** to deploy **5,000 electric trucks**, backed by investment of up to **₹150 crore**. At that scale, this is one of the larger electric commercial vehicle commitments announced in India this year.

## A sub-5-minute swap station near Mumbai

On 26 October, **Blue Energy Motors** and **HPCL** opened a **battery-swapping station for heavy trucks in Bhiwandi**, near Mumbai — with swaps completed in **under 5 minutes**. The station is positioned to serve the busy **Mumbai-Pune corridor**, one of India's most heavily trafficked freight routes. Fast swapping, rather than slow charging, is increasingly looking like the practical answer for heavy trucks that can't afford hours of downtime.

## Why trucking could be the next big EV opportunity

Separately, **Olectra's** Managing Director said on 27 October that **electric trucks represent the next big EV opportunity** in India — but stressed that **financing**, not technology, is the biggest hurdle. That lines up exactly with what we're seeing: the vehicles, the partnerships and even the swapping infrastructure are starting to show up. What's still missing at scale is affordable financing for fleet operators to actually buy in.

## The takeaway

Three different companies, three different approaches — a brand-new OEM entrant, a large-scale deployment deal, and dedicated swap infrastructure — all converging on electric trucks in the same week. If financing catches up the way battery-swap infrastructure just has, this could be the segment that moves fastest in India's EV story over the next couple of years.
