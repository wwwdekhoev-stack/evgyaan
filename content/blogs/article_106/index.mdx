The Bajaj Chetak C3001 is ₹1,16,483 and the [Ather Konarc](https://www.atherenergy.com/konarc) S 125 is ₹1,21,999, ex-showroom Bengaluru. The Chetak is ₹5,516 cheaper, carries a bigger battery, and claims slightly more range.

On paper the Konarc looks beaten. The charging section is where it fights back.

## The Chetak has the bigger pack

|  | Ather Konarc S 125 | Bajaj Chetak C3001 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price (ex-showroom, Bengaluru) | ₹1,21,999 | ₹1,16,483 |
| Battery | 2.7 kWh | 3.0 kWh |
| Claimed range | 125 km (IDC) | 127 km |
| Motor | 4.7 kW, 16 Nm | 3.1 kW |
| Boot space | 31 litres | — |

A note on the Chetak's figures: some retail listings show 127 km for the C3001, while Bajaj's updated C30 series listings show up to 131 km and a higher top speed following the mid-2026 refresh. Ask the dealer which specification the scooter on the floor is built to.

The Konarc gets within 2 km of the Chetak using 0.3 kWh less battery, and makes 1.6 kW more power doing it. That efficiency is the headline of Ather's new EL platform.

## Two metal-bodied scooters, two philosophies

Both of these have steel bodies, which is rarer than it should be in this segment and a genuine durability argument.

The Chetak is the traditional one — proven, familiar, backed by a dealer network that has now put [over 10 lakh Chetaks on the road](https://evgyan.com/blogs/bajaj-chetak-10-lakh-milestone).

The Konarc is the technical one — 14-inch front wheel, 7-inch dashboard on the S variants, 10,000 km service interval, up to an 8-year battery warranty.

That service interval deserves a second look. Double the usual gap between workshop visits, over four or five years of ownership, is real money saved.

## Charging and DC fast charging

Bajaj supplies a portable charger with the C3001 and quotes roughly 3 hours 30 minutes for 0-80%.

The Konarc's 450W charger is built into the scooter, so you carry a cable. On its own, 0-80% takes about 4 hours 30 minutes. With a 450W additional charger working alongside it, that drops to roughly 2 hours 25 minutes — quicker than the Chetak.

The bigger difference is away from home. The Konarc can use Ather Grid, which crossed over 5,000 public fast chargers nationwide as of Ather's January 2026 network update, with Ather claiming up to 15 km of range from 10 minutes plugged in. The Chetak has no DC fast-charging option at all.

## What this means for you

**If you charge overnight at home and nowhere else:** the C3001 is the sensible buy. ₹5,516 less, a bigger battery, quicker standard charging, and Bajaj's service reach.

**If you ever charge away from home — at work, at a mall, mid-ride:** the Konarc S 125 is worth the premium. Ather Grid is a capability the Chetak does not have and cannot add later.

**If you want the lowest running cost over several years:** the Konarc's 10,000 km service interval and longer battery warranty can outweigh the ₹5,516 upfront saving on the Chetak.

That is the honest split. It is not about which scooter is better. It is about where you plug in.
