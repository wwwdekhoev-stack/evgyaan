The River Indie carries a 4 kWh battery and claims 163 km. The [Ather Konarc](https://www.atherenergy.com/konarc) S 161 carries 3.5 kWh and claims 161 km. Two kilometres apart — and ₹12,200 apart.

The Konarc S 161 is ₹1,44,999 ex-showroom Bengaluru; the Indie is ₹1,57,199. Both are made in Bengaluru, which makes this a genuinely local fight.

## Half a kWh for nothing?

|  | Ather Konarc S 161 | River Indie |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price (ex-showroom, Bengaluru) | ₹1,44,999 | ₹1,57,199 |
| Battery | 3.5 kWh | 4 kWh |
| Claimed range | 161 km (IDC) | 163 km (IDC) |
| Top speed | 70 km/h | 90 km/h |
| Boot space | 31 litres | 55 litres |

The Indie uses 0.5 kWh more battery to go 2 km further. That is a straight efficiency win for the Konarc, and it is exactly what the new EL platform was built for.

But the Indie is not trying to win on efficiency. It is 20 km/h faster at the top end, and it has 55 litres of underseat storage against the Konarc's 31. That is the difference between fitting one helmet and fitting a week's groceries.

## Who each one is actually for

The Indie is a utility scooter. River designed it around carrying things — the boot, the pannier mounts, the flat floor. If you run errands, make deliveries, or routinely carry more than a bag, it is the more useful machine and the 55 litres settles it.

The Konarc is a commuter. Lighter on the battery, cheaper to buy, and tuned to stretch every kWh.

## Charging and DC fast charging

The Konarc S 161 gets a 450W additional charger as standard, working alongside the 450W charger built into the scooter for 900W combined. That is 0-80% in about 3 hours 10 minutes, or 5 hours 40 minutes on the built-in charger alone.

River supplies a 750W charger with the Indie and quotes roughly five hours for a full charge. River says the Indie supports fast charging, but it has no public fast-charging network of its own — riders depend on whatever third-party points exist nearby.

Ather does have that network. The Konarc can use Ather Grid, which crossed over 5,000 public fast chargers nationwide as of Ather's January 2026 network update, with up to 15 km of range claimed from 10 minutes plugged in. On a scooter this size, that is the difference between planning your day around the battery and not thinking about it.

## The price you see may not be the price you pay

Worth flagging: River's own website lists the Indie from about ₹1,46,799 because that figure includes the PM E-DRIVE benefit, while the Bengaluru ex-showroom listing is ₹1,57,199. Ask the dealer which number applies to you before you compare anything.

Karnataka offers no state EV subsidy, which is why we quote Bengaluru ex-showroom prices across our comparisons — there is nothing extra coming off the top.

## What this means for you

**If you are commuting:** buy the Konarc S 161. It is ₹12,200 cheaper, matches the Indie on claimed range from a smaller battery, and plugs into a fast-charging network the Indie cannot match.

**If you carry things — deliveries, errands, groceries:** buy the River Indie. 55 litres and 90 km/h are real advantages that no amount of efficiency makes up for, and ₹12,200 is a fair price for them.

**If budget is the deciding factor and charging access is good at home:** the Konarc still wins on pure value, matching the Indie's range for ₹12,200 less.

There is no wrong answer here. There is just a question: are you moving yourself, or moving stuff?

## FAQ

**Which is cheaper, the Ather Konarc S 161 or the River Indie?**
The Konarc S 161 is ₹12,200 cheaper — ₹1,44,999 against ₹1,57,199, both ex-showroom Bengaluru.

**Does the River Indie have more boot space than the Konarc?**
Yes, significantly — 55 litres against the Konarc S 161's 31 litres.

**Can the River Indie fast charge?**
River says the Indie supports fast charging, but it has no dedicated public fast-charging network of its own, unlike the Konarc's access to Ather Grid.

**Is the River Indie actually cheaper with the PM E-DRIVE benefit?**
River's own site lists the Indie from about ₹1,46,799 including the PM E-DRIVE benefit, versus the ₹1,57,199 Bengaluru ex-showroom figure — confirm which applies to you at the dealership.
