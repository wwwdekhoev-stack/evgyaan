Ather Energy is making a deliberate trade-off with its upcoming **Konarc** platform: and it's choosing margins over an early entry-level push.

On the Q2 FY27 analyst call on 26 October, Ather CEO Tarun Mehta said the company has **delayed the entry-level Konarc model** and will roll out the platform in phases, starting with **mid-range variants** in states such as Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan, to protect margins. [Autocar Professional](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/ather-flags-unmet-monthly-sales-of-20000-units-plans-capacity-expansion-135476) first reported the call.

## Why skip the cheap model first?

This is a notable strategic call, for a couple of reasons:

- Entry-level EVs typically carry the thinnest margins: they're priced to win volume and market share, not profit. Launching the mid-range version first means Ather gets to establish Konarc in the market at better unit economics before it eventually competes at the budget end.
- It also suggests Ather doesn't feel it needs an aggressive price-led push right now to defend its position: a reasonable read, given the company is simultaneously saying ([see our story on Ather's capacity gap](/blogs/ather-cant-meet-scooter-demand)) that it's **turning away 18,000-20,000 potential buyers a month** due to capacity constraints. If demand is already outstripping supply at current price points, there's less urgency to rush out a cheaper variant purely to chase volume.

## What this tells us about Ather's playbook

Taken together with its capacity comments, this looks like a company managing growth deliberately rather than chasing every possible sale. Ather appears to be choosing **sustainable, margin-healthy growth** over maximum volume at any cost: a stance that will please investors watching its path to profitability, even if it means budget-conscious buyers will have to wait longer for a cheaper Konarc variant.

It's also a reminder that "entry-level EV" announcements don't always mean an imminent launch: pricing strategy and margin protection can push even a planned budget model further down the roadmap.
