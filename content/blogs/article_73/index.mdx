Royal Enfield has a second electric motorcycle, and this one is built for bad roads. The Royal Enfield Flying Flea S6 was shown in near-production form at EICMA 2026 in Milan on Tuesday and goes on sale in summer 2027. The bike it shares a platform with, the Flying Flea C6, already sells in India at ₹2.79 lakh ex-showroom.

It is a scrambler and looks it. A 19-inch front wheel, an 18-inch rear, dual-purpose CEAT tyres, longer suspension travel, an inverted fork and a preload-adjustable monoshock. The battery sits inside a finned magnesium case that doubles as a heat sink. Colours are Dart Yellow and Lightning Grey.

## Six modes, and the one that counts

The S6 carries six riding modes. One of them is set up for dirt, and one the rider builds themselves. Regen braking and traction control are both adjustable. The ABS reads lean angle and switches off at both wheels, which is the setting that matters once the surface turns loose.

Instruments are a 3.5-inch round TFT touchscreen running Fleaware.OS, Royal Enfield's own software, on Qualcomm hardware. The bike locks and unlocks from a distance, shares where it is in real time, and pings a phone or watch if something is wrong.

What Royal Enfield did not announce: battery size, power, torque, range, kerb weight or price. B. Govindarajan, Managing Director of Eicher Motors and CEO of Royal Enfield, said only that the S6 "pushes the boundaries of urban exploration".

## The C6 tells you what to expect

Since the S6 runs on the C6's architecture, the C6's figures are the starting point:

- Battery: 3.91 kWh
- Claimed IDC range: 154 km
- Motor: 15.4 kW and 60 Nm
- Top speed: 115 kph
- Kerb weight: 124 kg
- Charging: 20-80% in 1 hour 5 minutes
- Price in India: ₹2.79 lakh, or ₹1.99 lakh with the battery on subscription

The S6 should cost more. Knobbly tyres, longer travel and a bigger front wheel all add to the bill, so expect it to sit above the C6.

## What this means for you

Here is the bit no press release mentions. Royal Enfield named both the C6 and the S6 at EICMA 2024, and said then that the S6 would follow the C6 closely. The C6 reached Indian buyers in April 2026. Summer 2027 is over a year behind it, and nearly three years after the name first went up on a show stand.

The upside is that nobody has run away with this segment yet. Electric motorcycles made up just 3,631 of India's 2,23,886 electric two-wheeler registrations in October 2026 on Vahan, or 1.6% (3,631 ÷ 2,23,886). Scooters are where the volume sits. An electric scrambler arriving in mid-2027 is not late to a full party, it is early to one that has barely started.

The S6 was one of four Royal Enfield machines in Milan and the only electric one. Ultraviolette, which premiered its Tesseract Maxi at the same show, should field the Shockwave against it.
