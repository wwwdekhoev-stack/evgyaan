The [Ather Konarc](https://www.atherenergy.com/konarc) S 100 is cheaper and goes further. ₹99,999 against ₹1,09,990 for the Vida VX2 Go 2.2 kWh, ex-showroom Bengaluru, and 100 km of claimed IDC range against 93 km.

₹9,991 less and 7 km more is the kind of result that usually ends a comparison. Here it does not, because of one thing the Vida does.

## The entry-level numbers

|  | Ather Konarc S 100 | Vida VX2 Go 2.2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price (ex-showroom, Bengaluru) | ₹99,999 | ₹1,09,990 |
| Battery | 2.1 kWh fixed | 2.2 kWh removable |
| Claimed range | 100 km (IDC) | 93 km (IDC) |
| Top speed | 70 km/h | 70 km/h |
| Motor | 4 kW, 16 Nm | — |
| Boot space | 31 litres | 33.2 litres |

Same top speed, almost the same battery, almost the same boot. The Konarc claims 7 km more and costs ₹9,991 less.

## The Vida's one big advantage

The VX2 Go's battery comes out. Carry it upstairs, plug it in, bring it back down. If your building has no socket near the parking, that single feature is worth more than range, price and power combined — because without it you cannot own the scooter at all.

Vida also offers the VX2 on a Battery-as-a-Service plan, where you pay less upfront and pay per kilometre for the battery. That lowers the entry price substantially, though it changes the running-cost maths, so work it out over your actual yearly distance before signing.

## Charging and fast charging

Vida quotes 0-80% in about 3 hours 7 minutes on the 580W charger supplied with the VX2 Go 2.2, which is quick for the segment. At Vida's own fast-charging points the company claims 0-80% in roughly an hour.

The Konarc's 450W charger is built into the scooter, so a plain cable replaces the brick. 0-80% takes about four hours on that alone, or roughly two hours with a 450W additional charger working alongside it.

On public fast charging, Ather has the larger footprint — Ather Grid had crossed over 5,000 chargers nationwide as of Ather's January 2026 network update, with up to 13 km of range claimed from a 10-minute stop on the S 100. Vida's network is real but smaller. Check which one actually has chargers on your route.

## The waiting problem

The Konarc S 100 is not in showrooms yet. Ather has placed entry-level deliveries in Q1 2027; the S 125 and S 161 started reaching buyers from mid-September 2026. The Vida VX2 Go is available now.

For a buyer who needs a scooter this month, that settles it regardless of the spec sheet.

## What this means for you

**If you can charge where you park and can wait until Q1 2027:** the Konarc S 100 is the better scooter for less money — cheaper, longer range, bigger fast-charging network.

**If you cannot charge where you park:** buy the Vida VX2 Go and do not think twice. ₹9,991 is a fair price for a battery you can carry inside.

**If you need a scooter this month:** the Vida VX2 Go is the only one of the two actually available right now.
