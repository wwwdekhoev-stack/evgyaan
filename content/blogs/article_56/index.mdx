Hero published no specs for its sharpest electric motorcycle yet at EICMA 2026. Its own dashboard gave away the only number anyone has: **150 km left** at 80% charge, in ECO mode, with 3 km on the odometer.

That is a display on a show bike, not a claim. Take it as a hint, nothing more.

## What the screen shows

The Vida Project VXZ gets a wide horizontal touchscreen, closer to a tablet than an instrument cluster. There is a search bar across the middle, a service reminder, a park function, and traction control and ABS symbols along the top.

The interesting bit sits at the bottom: two ride mode buttons marked **Street** and **Track**. The switchgear also carries an SOS control.

A Track mode on a company's first performance EV is not decoration.

## What the bike shows

Everything else has to be read off the machine, because Hero has said nothing.

- **Belt final drive**, motor sitting low near the swingarm pivot
- **Pirelli Diablo Supercorsa SC2 tyres**, which are track rubber, not commuter rubber
- Upside-down forks at the front, a monoshock and a raw cast swingarm at the back
- Discs at both ends, with a ByBre caliper up front (per Rushlane)
- Split rider and pillion seats, a high sharp tail, a slim tail light
- A projector headlamp inside a V-shaped LED signature
- A **charging socket under a hinged cover** on the tank-style panel

Not disclosed: battery size, motor power, torque, top speed, weight, charging standard, price, launch date.

## What this means for you

The VXZ is the one Vida concept built with **Zero Motorcycles**, the US performance EV maker Hero has partnered with since 2022. The UBX naked and the DIRT.E MX7 shown alongside it are Hero's own.

Hero is running two electric motorcycle platforms, UBEX and VXZ, with its first electric motorcycle targeted at **2027**. The VXZ is the more ambitious of the two, so expect the UBEX-based bike to reach showrooms first.

**The EVGyan take:** Vida sells scooters today, close to 30,000 a month. Commuter businesses do not fit Supercorsa tyres and a Track mode by accident. This is Hero telling us it wants a second kind of buyer. A concept costs nothing though, so the VXZ gets our attention, not our verdict. We will judge it the day Hero publishes a kW figure, a kWh figure and a price.
