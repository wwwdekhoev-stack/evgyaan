TVS dispatched 71,382 electric two-wheelers in October 2026, more than double the 32,387 it sent out in the same month last year. That makes it the company's biggest electric month yet, and it sat inside a total of 700,134 vehicles, up 28.81% year on year.

Work out the share and the number gets more interesting. Electric two-wheelers were 10.2% of everything TVS dispatched last month (our calculation: 71,382 of 700,134). A year ago that share was just under 6%.

## Dispatches are not registrations

One number needs a caveat. Dispatches are what a company sends to dealers and export markets. Vahan registrations are what buyers actually took home in India. In October, Vahan recorded 60,433 TVS electric two-wheeler registrations against those 71,382 dispatches, a gap of 10,949.

Two things explain it, and neither is bad news. The dispatch figure includes export shipments, and TVS's total international business grew 51% to 175,350 units in October. The rest is dealer stock. Diwali falls on 8 November this year, so October was the month to fill showrooms, not to empty them.

That matters for what comes next. Scooters sitting in a dealership in October become registrations in November.

## The capacity ceiling is getting close

On its 29 October results call, TVS said it can build 60,000 to 65,000 electric two-wheelers a month right now, and wants about 80,000 a month by the end of March 2027. October's 71,382 dispatches are already above the top of that stated band, which usually means stock being drawn down, overtime, or both.

The rest of October's numbers:

- Two-wheelers of every kind: 670,679 against 525,150 a year ago, up 28%
- Of those, sold in India: 514,764, a 22% rise
- Of those, electric: 71,382, up 120%
- Autos and other three-wheelers: 29,455, a 60% rise
- Everything shipped overseas: 175,350, up 51%

Sequentially the electric number grew 8.5% over September's 65,799, so this is a steady climb rather than a festive spike.

## What this means for you

If you are waiting on an iQube or an Orbiter, October was the month your dealer got stock. Ask about colours and variants now, before Diwali buyers clear the floor in the second week of November.

The wider point: TVS registered 60,433 of India's 2,23,886 electric two-wheelers in October, roughly 27% of the market. A brand holding a quarter of the segment and doubling its output in a year does not have a demand problem. It has a factory problem, and it has already told the market it is spending to fix that.
