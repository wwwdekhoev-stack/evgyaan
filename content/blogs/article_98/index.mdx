There will be an electric Land Rover Defender, and it starts coming off the line in 2027. JLR confirmed it alongside its Q2 results on 13 November, and the car will be built at Halewood in England on the EMA platform, next to the electric Range Rover GT.

Here is the part most people have missed. The electric Defender is not a battery version of the Defender 90 you can buy today. It is a new, smaller model that sits below the 90 in the range, measuring just over 4.5 metres. Land Rover has had three Defender body styles since 2020. This makes four.

## JLR has not shown it, or even named it

No photos, no name. "Defender Sport" and "Defender 80" are both in circulation, and neither came from JLR. The company has also kept back the power output, the battery size, the range and the exact dimensions. On JLR's Q2 earnings call, CEO PB Balaji put the timing simply: "the [Range Rover] GT and the Defender electric start off in Halewood next year."

What is confirmed is where it comes from, and that matters more than it sounds. Today's Defender 90, 110 and 130 are built in Nitra, Slovakia. The electric one moves to Halewood, Merseyside, taking the slot the Range Rover Evoque gives up when that car ends production later in 2027.

A hybrid version of the same car is being worked on, but Balaji has said it will not arrive with the EV.

## The Tata angle

JLR is owned by Tata Motors, so this is an Indian-owned company putting its most recognisable badge on an electric platform. The quarter it was announced in was not a comfortable one: revenue up 20.3% year on year to £5.9 billion, but a post-tax loss of £451 million after £530 million of exceptional items, most of it restructuring. For the full year, JLR now guides to an adjusted operating margin of 2 to 3%, while planning to invest £15 to 18 billion, of which £11.5 billion has gone out of the door since 2024.

In other words, JLR is spending heavily on electrification while the numbers are tight. The Defender is the badge it can least afford to get wrong.

## What this means for you

India is not in the announcement. JLR has said nothing about when, or whether, the electric Defender reaches Indian showrooms, and a Halewood-built car landing here would come in as a CBU unless that changes. The Range Rover Electric, Range Rover Sport Electric and Jaguar Type 01 are further ahead, with customer deliveries due from early 2027.

If you are waiting for an electric version of the Defender you already know, this is not it. JLR has confirmed only this one electric Defender so far, and it is the smallest the brand has built. A battery-powered 110 or 130 is not on any timeline the company has put in writing.
