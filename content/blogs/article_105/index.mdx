The [Ather Konarc](https://www.atherenergy.com/konarc) S 161 is ₹7,141 cheaper than the Bajaj Chetak C3501 and claims 8 km more range. ₹1,44,999 against ₹1,52,140, ex-showroom Bengaluru, with 161 km against 153 km.

That is the top of both line-ups meeting head on, and it is closer than the price suggests.

## Same battery, different character

|  | Ather Konarc S 161 | Bajaj Chetak C3501 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price (ex-showroom, Bengaluru) | ₹1,44,999 | ₹1,52,140 |
| Battery | 3.5 kWh | 3.5 kWh |
| Claimed range | 161 km (IDC) | 153 km |
| Top speed | 70 km/h | 80 km/h |
| Motor | 4.7 kW, 16 Nm | 3.1 kW, 20 Nm |
| Boot space | 31 litres | — |

Identical 3.5 kWh packs. The Konarc squeezes 8 km more out of it and makes 1.6 kW more power. The Chetak pulls harder off the line with 20 Nm and runs 10 km/h faster at the top.

Both are metal-bodied, which still matters to a lot of buyers who do not trust plastic panels on Indian roads.

## Charging

The Konarc S 161 ships with a 450W additional charger as standard. It works alongside the 450W charger built into the scooter for 900W combined, giving 0-80% in about 3 hours 10 minutes. On the built-in charger alone it is 5 hours 40 minutes — so take the additional charger seriously, it is not an accessory you skip.

The onboard charger also means you travel with a cable rather than a brick, which is a small thing until you have carried a charger up to a third-floor flat.

Bajaj quotes about three hours for a full charge on the C3501 with its portable charger. On like-for-like wall charging, the Chetak is the quicker of the two.

## DC fast charging is the Konarc's trump card

The Chetak has no DC fast-charging option. A wall socket is the whole story.

The Konarc can use Ather Grid — which crossed over 5,000 public fast chargers nationwide as of Ather's January 2026 network update, with Ather claiming up to 15 km of range from a 10-minute stop. For anyone without reliable parking-spot charging, that is not a nice-to-have. It is the reason to buy.

## Bajaj's counter-argument is scale

Chetak is not a small player playing catch-up. The range just crossed [10 lakh registrations](https://evgyan.com/blogs/bajaj-chetak-10-lakh-milestone), and Bajaj's service network reaches towns where Ather has not arrived yet.

If you live outside a metro, that reach is worth more than a spec sheet. A scooter you can get fixed locally beats a scooter that is 8 km better on paper.

## What this means for you

**If you are in a metro with Ather Grid coverage:** take the Konarc S 161. It wins on paper and on price — ₹7,141 less, 8 km more, more power, 31 litres of boot, and a fast-charging network behind it.

**If you ride outside a metro, or value Bajaj's wider service reach:** take the Chetak C3501. It wins on speed, charge time from a wall socket, and the one thing no spec sheet shows: how far you have to ride to get it serviced.

**If raw acceleration matters more than range:** the Chetak's 20 Nm torque figure gives it the edge off the line, even though the Konarc has more peak power.
