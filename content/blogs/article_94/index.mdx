The electric Audi A4 has a new date, and it is not the one Audi kept repeating. Audi A4 e-tron production will now begin in March 2029 at the earliest, a full year after the 2028 start that Audi's technical chief Rouven Mohr was still standing behind this July. CEO Gernot Döllner has put March 2029 on the calendar, and people close to the project say it could drift to late April or May.

Nothing is wrong with the car itself. It is waiting for the platform underneath it.

## A platform that keeps missing its train

The A4 e-tron will ride on the Volkswagen Group's Scalable Systems Platform, or SSP. VW first showed SSP in July 2021 and promised the first car on it by 2026. The idea was one 800-volt base to replace the 400-volt MEB (the Q4 e-tron's platform) and the 800-volt PPE (A6 e-tron, Q6 e-tron). If the A4 e-tron ends up as the first SSP car, SSP lands roughly three years behind its own schedule.

Software ate a big chunk of that time. Cariad, VW's in-house software unit, never delivered the SSP software, so VW teamed up with Rivian in June 2024. That joint venture built the new software layer, SDV 1.0, for VW Group cars sold outside China.

## Why 48 volts changed the plan

An EV runs two electrical systems. The big one, 800 volts here, powers the motors. A smaller network feeds everything else: control units, sensors, screens, locks. Almost every car on sale, VW's included, uses 12 volts for that second job.

Döllner wanted the A4 e-tron on 48 volts instead. Four times the voltage means slimmer wiring and more headroom for a modern car's many computers. The catch: SDV 1.0 was drawn up for 12 volts, so it has to be reworked, and parts suppliers have to retool along with it.

Audi is also asking for an A4 that feels like an A4 inside. That means proper footwells for each rear passenger instead of a completely flat floor, which sends engineers back to work out where the battery and high-voltage parts should go. Audi plans both saloon and Avant estate versions, with styling expected to draw on the Concept C that Audi showed in 2025.

## The EVGyan take

A year is a long time in this segment. BMW's Neue Klasse i3 and the electric Mercedes C-Class now get a longer run at buyers before the most familiar name in the class turns up.

Still, this is the sort of delay you would rather see before launch than after it. Tesla's Cybertruck already runs on a 48-volt network, and the industry is heading that way. Getting it right on the first SSP car gives every later VW Group model a cleaner base. Audi is not standing still meanwhile either: it began building the compact A2 e-tron at Ingolstadt in September.

## What this means for buyers in India

The A4 in Indian Audi showrooms today is petrol only, and Audi has not said anything about bringing the A4 e-tron here. With production starting in 2029, an Indian launch would come later still.
