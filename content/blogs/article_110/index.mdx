₹18,301 for 10 km. That is the arithmetic between the [Ather Konarc](https://www.atherenergy.com/konarc) S 161 at ₹1,44,999, claiming 161 km, and the Bajaj Chetak C3503 at ₹1,26,698, claiming 151 km — both 3.5 kWh, both ex-showroom Bengaluru.

Put like that, the Chetak looks like the obvious buy. It mostly is, with one caveat.

## Same battery, ₹18,301 apart

|  | Ather Konarc S 161 | Bajaj Chetak C3503 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price (ex-showroom, Bengaluru) | ₹1,44,999 | ₹1,26,698 |
| Battery | 3.5 kWh | 3.5 kWh |
| Claimed range | 161 km (IDC) | 151 km |
| Top speed | 70 km/h | 70 km/h after the 2026 update |
| Motor | 4.7 kW, 16 Nm | 3.1 kW, 20 Nm |
| Boot space | 31 litres | — |

The C3503 is the value pick of Bajaj's 3.5 kWh trio — it uses the same pack as the C3501 and C3502 but with a colour LCD instead of a touchscreen TFT and drum brakes instead of disc. For most buyers that is ₹25,000 of screen they will not miss.

Note on the Chetak's top speed: earlier listings for the C3503 showed 63 km/h, while Bajaj's updated listings show 70 km/h after the 2026 refresh. Check which specification the showroom is selling.

## Charging

Bajaj quotes about 3 hours 25 minutes for 0-80% on the C3503 with its portable charger.

The Konarc S 161 includes a 450W additional charger as standard, which pairs with the 450W charger built into the scooter for 900W combined and 0-80% in roughly 3 hours 10 minutes. Close enough that it should not sway you.

The Konarc's onboard charger is still the nicer piece of design — a cable rather than a brick to carry around.

## DC fast charging is what the ₹18,301 actually buys

The Chetak C3503 has no DC fast-charging option.

The Konarc can use Ather Grid — which crossed over 5,000 public fast chargers nationwide as of Ather's January 2026 network update, with Ather claiming up to 15 km of range from 10 minutes plugged in.

So the honest way to frame this comparison is not "₹18,301 for 10 km". It is "₹18,301 for 10 km, 1.6 kW more power, a 10,000 km service interval, and access to a national fast-charging network". Framed that way it is a much closer call.

## Bajaj's scale argument

The Chetak range just passed [10 lakh registrations](https://evgyan.com/blogs/bajaj-chetak-10-lakh-milestone), and Bajaj's dealer network reaches places Ather has not opened in yet. If you are outside a metro, that alone can settle it.

## What this means for you

**If you charge at home and ride in a town without Ather Grid coverage:** buy the Chetak C3503. ₹18,301 is a lot of money to pay for a network you cannot reach, and 151 km is plenty.

**If you are in a metro and sometimes need a top-up mid-day:** the Konarc S 161 earns the premium.

**If you want the cheapest 3.5 kWh scooter on this list:** the Chetak C3503 is it, and Bajaj's dealer reach is the wider of the two.

Check your own map first — that is what decides this one, not the spec sheet.
