₹4,009 buys 19 km of claimed range. The [Ather Konarc](https://www.atherenergy.com/konarc) S 161 is ₹1,44,999 and claims 161 km; the Vida VX2 Plus 3.4 kWh is ₹1,40,990 and claims 142 km, both ex-showroom Bengaluru.

But the Vida has one feature the Konarc cannot match at any price: you can take the battery out.

| Top speed | 70 km/h | 80 km/h |

Nearly identical battery size, 19 km apart on claimed range. The Vida is 10 km/h faster at the top.

## The removable battery changes who can buy

If you live in a flat with no charging point in the parking area — which describes a very large share of Indian cities — the Vida solves a problem the Konarc does not. You lift the pack out, carry it upstairs, and plug it in at home.

No amount of range makes up for not being able to charge at all. For a lot of buyers, this is the entire decision.

The flip side: the pack is heavy, and carrying it up and down every night gets old fast. Be honest with yourself about which floor you live on.

## Charging and fast charging

Vida quotes 0-80% in about 4 hours 44 minutes on the 580W charger that comes with the VX2 Plus 3.4. Vida also has its own fast-charging points, where the company claims 0-80% in roughly an hour — a meaningful step up.

The Konarc S 161 gets a 450W additional charger as standard, working alongside the 450W charger built into the scooter for 900W combined and 0-80% in about 3 hours 10 minutes. On home charging alone, the Konarc is the quicker of the two.

On public fast charging, the question is coverage. Ather Grid had crossed over 5,000 chargers nationwide as of Ather's January 2026 network update, with up to 15 km of range claimed per 10-minute stop. Vida's network exists but is smaller. Check what is actually near you rather than going by the headline count.

## Both brands are scaling hard

Neither of these is a side project. Hero's Vida and Ather are both chasing big monthly volumes, and the capacity race across Bajaj, TVS and Vida is one we have been tracking in [the electric scooter capacity race](https://evgyan.com/blogs/electric-scooter-capacity-race-india).

That is good news for buyers. More supply usually means better offers at the dealership.

## What this means for you

**If you have a plug point where you park:** take the Konarc S 161. 19 km more claimed range, faster home charging, a bigger fast-charging network, and ₹4,009 is not much for all of that.

**If you do not have a plug where you park:** take the Vida VX2 Plus. The removable battery is not a feature, it is the thing that makes EV ownership possible for you — and the 80 km/h top speed is a bonus.

**If you want the cheaper scooter outright:** the Vida VX2 Plus also costs ₹4,009 less, so budget-first buyers without a home charging constraint still have a reasonable case for it.

## FAQ

**Can you remove the battery from the Vida VX2 Plus?**
Yes, the VX2 Plus 3.4 uses a removable battery pack that can be carried indoors to charge, unlike the Konarc S 161's fixed battery.

**Which has more range, the Konarc S 161 or the Vida VX2 Plus?**
The Konarc S 161 claims 161 km IDC against 142 km for the Vida VX2 Plus 3.4 — a 19 km gap for ₹4,009 more.

**Does the Vida VX2 Plus support fast charging?**
Yes, Vida operates its own fast-charging points where it claims 0-80% in roughly an hour, in addition to the standard 580W home charger.

**Which scooter is faster, the Konarc S 161 or the Vida VX2 Plus?**
The Vida VX2 Plus tops out at 80 km/h against the Konarc S 161's 70 km/h.
