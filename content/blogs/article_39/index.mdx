Who won October 2026, TVS or Bajaj? It depends on what you count.

In electric two-wheelers, **TVS won by 11,365**. In electric three-wheelers, **Bajaj won by 10,658**. Add the two together and TVS comes out on top by just **707 vehicles**: 65,500 against 64,793.

## Round 2: Three-wheelers belong to Bajaj

Bajaj has been ahead of TVS in electric three-wheelers in every month of 2026, and October was no different: 15,725 against 5,067. Bajaj has also been the number one brand in this segment for three months running. Read the [full three-wheeler report](/blogs/electric-three-wheeler-sales-october-2026).

TVS is not standing still here. Its three-wheeler registrations grew 69.8% over last year. But Bajaj's grew 91.3%, so the gap is widening in absolute numbers, not narrowing.

This segment is why Bajaj stays ahead on the FY27 scoreboard. Over April to October, Bajaj registered 93,775 three-wheelers against TVS's 30,395. That cushion of 63,380 is bigger than TVS's two-wheeler lead of 48,160 over the same stretch.

## Two different bets

The two companies have built their EV businesses differently:

- **TVS:** 92.3% of its EV registrations are two-wheelers. It is a scooter-first business.
- **Bajaj:** 75.7% are two-wheelers and 24.3% are three-wheelers. It has a second leg to stand on.

Both have almost exactly doubled over FY27. TVS grew 98.42% and Bajaj 98.43%, from 1,92,189 and 1,99,850 a year ago. Between them, the two companies registered 1,30,293 EVs in October, which is 36.8% of every EV registered in India.

Both are now adding production capacity. TVS wants to [reach 80,000 electric scooters a month](/blogs/tvs-electric-scooter-capacity-expansion) and Bajaj is aiming at [around 80,000 Chetaks a month](/blogs/bajaj-chetak-production-80000-target).

## What this means for you

- **If you are buying a scooter:** both brands are the two most-registered in India, so service reach is not the problem it was a few years ago. Compare delivery dates and local service, not just the brochure.
- **If you are buying a three-wheeler for work:** Bajaj has the bigger lead there, 15,725 against 5,067 in October.
- **If you follow the market:** the real contest is not over a single month. Watch the FY27 gap of 15,217, which is smaller than a month of either brand's two-wheeler volume.

## The EVGyan take

October gave TVS the headline, and the headline is fair. TVS is the faster-growing brand in electric two-wheelers by a wide margin, and that is where most EVs are sold. But Bajaj's three-wheeler business is a real cushion, and it is the reason the year-to-date table still shows Bajaj in front.
