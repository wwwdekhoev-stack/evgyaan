93 km/h against 70 km/h. That is the headline of this comparison, and it costs ₹8,000. The [Ather Konarc](https://www.atherenergy.com/konarc) S 125 is ₹1,21,999; the Ampere Nexus EX+ is ₹1,29,999, ex-showroom Bengaluru.

The Nexus also claims 11 km more range. So what is the Konarc doing here?

## The Nexus wins the spec sheet

|  | Ather Konarc S 125 | Ampere Nexus EX+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price (ex-showroom, Bengaluru) | ₹1,21,999 | ₹1,29,999 |
| Battery | 2.7 kWh | 3 kWh LFP |
| Claimed range | 125 km (IDC) | 136 km |
| Top speed | 70 km/h | 93 km/h |
| Motor | 4.7 kW, 16 Nm | 4 kW peak |
| Boot space | 31 litres | 22 litres |

The Nexus is the fastest scooter in any of our Konarc comparisons, and 93 km/h from a 3 kWh family scooter is genuinely impressive. Ampere also quotes a real-world figure of about 100 km, which is honest of them and matches Ather's TrueRange claim for the S 125 exactly.

So in the real world, both do roughly 100 km. One of them is ₹8,000 cheaper and has 9 litres more boot.

## LFP is the Nexus's quiet advantage

The Nexus uses an LFP battery, which typically handles heat and charge cycles better than the NMC chemistry common in this segment. Ampere backs it with a 5-year/75,000 km battery warranty.

Ather counters with up to an 8-year battery warranty on the Konarc and a 10,000 km service interval. Both are strong ownership stories; read the fine print on what each warranty actually covers.

## Charging and fast charging

Ampere ships a 15A charger with the Nexus and quotes about 3 hours 20 minutes. A faster 25A charger is sold as an accessory, so ask what it costs before you assume the quick time applies to you.

The Konarc has a 450W charger built in — cable, not brick — taking about 4 hours 30 minutes for 0-80% alone, or roughly 2 hours 25 minutes with a 450W additional charger working alongside it.

On public DC fast charging, the Konarc stands alone here. Ather Grid had crossed over 5,000 chargers nationwide as of Ather's January 2026 network update, with up to 15 km of range claimed per 10-minute stop. Ampere has no equivalent network.

## What this means for you

**If top speed is what you are shopping for:** buy the Nexus EX+. 93 km/h is not a number anything else in this price band gets near, and the LFP pack is a sensible long-term choice.

**If you want the better all-round deal:** the Konarc S 125 is ₹8,000 less, matches the Nexus on real-world range, has 9 litres more boot, and plugs into a fast-charging network. The 70 km/h ceiling is the price you pay — and in city traffic, you will rarely touch it anyway.

**If long-term battery chemistry matters to you:** the Nexus's LFP pack is generally considered more heat-tolerant than NMC, which can matter over many Indian summers.

## FAQ

**Is the Ather Konarc S 125 cheaper than the Ampere Nexus EX+?**
Yes, by ₹8,000 — ₹1,21,999 against ₹1,29,999, both ex-showroom Bengaluru.

**What top speed does the Ampere Nexus EX+ claim?**
93 km/h, which is 23 km/h faster than the Konarc S 125's 70 km/h — the highest top speed in any Konarc comparison so far.
