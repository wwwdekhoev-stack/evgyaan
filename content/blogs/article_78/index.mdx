BYD has crossed 5,000 customer deliveries for the Sealion 7 in India, and the cheapest way into one now costs ₹41.90 lakh ex-showroom. For a car in that bracket, that is a real number.

Deliveries have been running since February 2025. Spread across the 21 months to the end of October, 5,000-plus cars works out to roughly 240 a month. Nobody is going to call that a volume seller. But put it next to the price tag and it looks different: a ₹42 lakh-and-up electric SUV finding a few hundred buyers every month, through 49 showrooms in 40 cities.

## The ₹41.90 lakh version changed the math

For most of its life here the Sealion 7 was a ₹50 lakh car. That changed in August 2026, when BYD added the Dynamic variant as the new entry point at ₹41.90 lakh ex-showroom, calling it an introductory price. It uses a smaller 71.8 kWh Blade Battery and claims 520 km on the NEDC cycle.

Then came October and a Festive Edition at ₹50.60 lakh, built on the Premium variant and capped at 150 cars. Tan and black cabin, quilted Nappa leather, electric sunshade, head-up display. A limited run like that says BYD still sees appetite at the top of the range, not only at the bottom.

Rajeev Chauhan, who heads BYD India's electric passenger vehicle business, called the Sealion 7 "one of our strongest-performing models in India".

## What you get for the money

- Dynamic: 71.8 kWh battery, 520 km claimed NEDC range, ₹41.90 lakh
- Premium: 82.56 kWh battery, 567 km claimed NEDC range
- Performance: 82.56 kWh, dual motors, 390 kW and 690 Nm, 0-100 kmph in 4.5 seconds
- Festive Edition: 150 units only, ₹50.60 lakh
- Every variant: panoramic glass roof, 15.6-inch rotating touchscreen

Charging runs at 11 kW on AC and up to 150 kW on DC, or about 45 minutes for a fast-charge stop. Worth saying plainly: NEDC is an optimistic cycle. Expect less in Indian traffic with the AC running.

## The EVGyan take

Five thousand cars in 21 months is not a win on volume. India registered more than 36,000 electric cars in October alone. It is a win on positioning. BYD has built a premium EV base here without a local manufacturing approval going its way, and the Sealion 7 did most of that work.

The interesting part is what comes next. BYD brings its luxury brand Denza to India on 13 November, pushing the group further upmarket. The Sealion 7 is about to stop being BYD's flagship story here, and on the evidence of these 5,000 cars it has earned the promotion.

## What this means for you

If you are shopping above ₹40 lakh, the Dynamic is the variant worth a test drive, and the introductory tag on its price is a reason not to wait too long. If you want the bigger 82.56 kWh battery, ask about the Premium, and have that conversation before Denza lands and showroom attention moves.
